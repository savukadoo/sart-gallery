# SART Gallery remote-control review

Scope: Samsung Tizen web app on the Samsung test model. The apple-design skill supplied general accessibility, focus, selection, layout and modal-dismissal principles; Apple-specific platform conventions are not applied to Samsung controls.

The screen's job is to let someone adjust their art comfortably using only the small TV remote. The visual language remains charcoal surfaces, warm ivory text and restrained bronze accents.

## Findings and changes

- Critical: native dropdowns consumed directional keys, so Fit could prevent reaching Done. Settings had the same native-control dependency. Replaced Fit with two visible preview buttons and Settings dropdowns with large value buttons opening short choice lists (`app/main.mjs:226`, `app/main.mjs:235`). All options can be reached with Up/Down and activated with OK.
- High: duplicate focus identifiers made the header close control and Done ambiguous. Dialog actions now have distinct identifiers; Fit and brightness explicitly route Down to Done (`app/main.mjs:226`).
- High: leaving a setting needed a predictable destination. Selecting an option, cancelling, or pressing Back now returns focus to the edited setting (`app/main.mjs:209`, `app/main.mjs:235`).
- Medium: slideshow state and its long delay were difficult to discover. Artwork controls now show whether the slideshow is paused or running, with the chosen duration (`app/main.mjs:143`).
- Medium: native brightness and checkbox controls depended on keyboard conventions. Brightness and contrast sliders now explicitly consume Left/Right for adjustment, route Up/Down between controls and route OK to Done; resume uses an explicit On/Off button (`app/main.mjs:448`). Selection has both a check mark and a border, and focus has a separate light outline.

## Reference principles

- `focus-and-selection.md` > Best practices: every onscreen element must be reachable with directional inputs, and focus should not move unexpectedly.
- `modality.md` > Best practices: "Always give people an obvious way to dismiss a modal view."
- `accessibility.md` > Vision: "Convey information with more than color alone."
- `segmented-controls.md` > Best practices: group related choices and clearly show their selected state.
- `app-icons.md` > Design: keep a simple recognizable composition, inspect small sizes and keep the core identity consistent across appearances.

## Evidence and limits

The latest release adds five original frame effects, nine mat choices including muted and antique colours, a rounded-safe icon, and shared Picture and Slideshow tabs. The Settings shell now reserves rows for the header, content and footer inside one viewport (`app/styles.css:61`). The photo library and About card share aligned edges. Twenty-nine native checks passed for 0.1.5; the final run preserved the operator's one-minute delay, 65% brightness and 120% contrast. The app default remains 5 minutes.

This is targeted regression evidence, not an assertion that every physical-remote path, long viewing session, storage limit, launcher mask or Samsung accessibility service has been accepted. Frame effects are rendered locally in CSS. Their palette references and their relationship to Samsung's mats and physical bezels are recorded in `frame-references.md`.
