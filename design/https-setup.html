iPhone or iPad

  1. AirDrop SART-TV-Trust.mobileconfig from this Mac to your device. This profile contains only the public TV certificate.
  2. On the device, open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and install the SART Gallery TV certificate profile. Check the certificate name and fingerprint above.
  3. Go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings. Enable full trust for {{CERT_NAME}}.
  4. On the TV, choose Add photos → Connect to my Mobile. Scan the new QR code and approve your device on the TV.

Apple’s certificate trust instructions

This Mac

  1. Open SART-TV-Trust.cer yourself and import it into your login keychain.
  2. In Keychain Access, open the certificate named above. Check its SHA-256 fingerprint, expand Trust and choose Always Trust for Secure Sockets Layer (SSL). Save and authenticate when macOS asks.
  3. Open Add photos → Connect to my PC on the TV, then visit the HTTPS address and enter the current code.

Apple’s Keychain Access instructions

Windows or Android

Import the public certificate file using your device’s trusted certificate settings. Windows: Current User → Trusted Root Certification Authorities. Android: Security → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate (wording varies). Browser support still requires a check on the actual device.

If the TV’s address changes

The certificate is restricted to {{TV_IP}}. Keep that address reserved in your router if possible. A changed address requires a new certificate authority and repeating device trust setup. The app will stop the connection instead of falling back to HTTP.

Remove trust later

Remove this SART profile from VPN & Device Management on iPhone/iPad, or remove this named certificate from your computer’s trust store. Your gallery photos stay on the TV.

Keep .local/https private on the Mac: it contains the authority signing key. Share only the public files in this folder. No authority signing key was sent to the TV or bundled in the app.
