# Global appearance — 0.3.0

Open Settings → Frame & mat for all artwork → Choose look. Frame and Mat tabs edit a draft, with a live artwork preview. The Mat tab includes Flat/Bevelled paper edge and nine colours. Cancel or Back discards the draft. Apply to all artwork saves the chosen frame, mat colour and paper edge as global defaults for all current and future pictures.

Application removes existing per-artwork overrides only for frame, matte and matStyle. It preserves per-artwork fit/crop and any unrelated look metadata. It also preserves slideshow settings, brightness, contrast, favourites and PC pairing. Preferences and trimmed look overrides are committed in one settings transaction. Individual artwork controls can establish new per-artwork overrides afterwards.

The Settings appearance control retains focus key setting-matte, so the existing Down route continues to Connect to my PC. The global dialog has exactly two tabs. Down from the mat finish reaches the selected colour, then the lower colour rows and Apply. Apply has a Left route to Cancel. Selection and focus indicators stay separate. Existing per-artwork dialogs retain their five tabs.

The preceding publication audit is in publication-review-2026-10-28.html and applies to the audited 0.2.2 snapshot. This feature does not resolve those store-submission gaps.
