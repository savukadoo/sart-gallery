How it works

Keep this page and SART Gallery open while sending, with both devices on the same home Wi-Fi. No companion or account is needed.

The TV serves this upload page and pairing over HTTPS. Photos travel directly to the TV over an encrypted connection. First-time certificate downloads use a separate setup page; uploads stay on HTTPS.

Choose up to 50 photos, each under 25 MB. JPEG, PNG and WebP work; HEIC depends on your browser. If a photo won’t open, export it as JPEG.

Display copies fit within 3840 × 2160 and omit location metadata. Your originals stay on your device.
