# Artwork and catalogue rights

The AGPLv3 licence for SART's software does not relicense the artwork,
catalogue records or user photos it displays. Keep their original rights
information and source credits when redistributing them.

## Included artwork

There are 60 included works: 26 from the Art Institute of Chicago, 23 from
the Cleveland Museum of Art and 11 from Artvee. Individual titles, artists,
dates, source URLs, rights/policy links and image checksums are recorded in
[app/assets/provenance.json](app/assets/provenance.json).

- The 49 museum reproductions are recorded as CC0/public-domain images.
  Their policies are [Art Institute Open Access](https://www.artic.edu/open-access/open-access-images)
  and [Cleveland Open Access](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access).
- The 11 Artvee reproductions are recorded as **Public domain (Artvee)**,
  rather than CC0. [Artvee's terms](https://artvee.com/terms-of-service/)
  and all 11 individual pages were retrieved and checked on 1 November
  2026. The terms permit downloading, sharing and reusing the images,
  including commercially. Each page marks the matching file as public
  domain in the country of origin and life-plus-70-or-shorter territories.
  The recorded artists died between 1931 and 1944; the Australian term
  assessment supports use there. Proun's 1923 portfolio publication is
  also corroborated by museum records. See the [individual checks and
  sources](app/assets/artvee-rights-review.json).

The provider-permission check has passed for all 11 images. This is not a
blanket worldwide copyright clearance. Individual US publication histories
have not been established: the year a painting was created is not enough
to establish its US public-domain status. See [US copyright duration](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap3.html)
and [Australia's copyright-term rules](https://www.infrastructure.gov.au/sites/default/files/fact_sheet_copyright_terms_0.pdf).

These records describe the source providers' rights labels; they do not
claim that SART owns the artworks or that a public-domain label clears
every possible right in every jurisdiction. Source-site and catalogue
metadata terms remain separate from image copyright.

## Downloaded art and user photos

Find art retains the source and rights information supplied by the selected
provider. New sources and images need their own rights checks; adding a
source does not place its content under AGPL or grant permission to scrape
its website. User uploads remain subject to their owners' rights. Uploading
an image does not grant other SART users permission to redistribute it.

## Application assets

SART's original UI assets, including the app icon source and its rendered
icon, are covered by the project's AGPL-3.0-only grant. This does not cover
third-party artwork, logos or screenshots embedded in reference materials.
